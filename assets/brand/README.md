# Brand assets

Supplied by the owner. Use exactly as supplied.

The supplied Attenlume logo artwork. These files are the masters: use them exactly as they are.

- `attenlume-logo-horizontal.png` (2172 × 724, transparent background): blue tile with the A, then "Attenlume" ("Atten" navy, "lume" blue) and "by Samlytics Data Services". Headers on wide screens, documents, slides, website, email signatures.
- `attenlume-logo-stacked.png` (1254 × 1254, transparent background): the same, stacked. Splash screens, posters, square spaces.

The long cream stroke that curls over the lime light is intentional. Do not shorten, close or redraw it. Place both lockups on cream or white. Never retype the wordmark.

Still needed from the designer, made from this same artwork: SVG masters, and the cream-tile and blue-tile icon versions shown on the brand board.

## App icon

- `attenlume-app-icon-primary.png` (1254 × 1254, transparent around a rounded navy tile): cream A, lime light. The primary app icon and the mark on its own, used as supplied.

Platform exports must be made from this same artwork, without changing it:

- iOS: 1024 × 1024, full-bleed navy square, no transparency and no pre-rounded corners. iOS applies its own mask, and App Store Connect rejects icons with transparency.
- Android: adaptive icon as a foreground layer (the A and light inside the 66dp safe zone of a 108dp canvas) on a navy (#00212B) background layer, plus a single-colour monochrome layer for themed icons.
- Windows: `.ico` with 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256px sizes.
